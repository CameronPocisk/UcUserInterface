## Interview Results

0. Tell me about your smart pen
    Users tended to think about notes when thinknig about their pen. Others who drew spoke about its features and pairing with a drawing tablet. 
1. What makes a smart pen feel high quality
    There was a large variety of answers here. In general, users like simple writling-like features.
2. What are your favorite features on a Smart Pen 
    Gesture controls /quick options were basically everybodies favorite feature. 
3. What problems do you face with your smart Pen
    Note users found small details, like alternate use or battery nitpicks to be slightly annoying. Note users seem to have basically no problems using their pens. For artists, they found that the line being different from the pens action was very annoying. 
5. Why do you decide to use a smart Pen (Vs normal pen)
    Note users like the convience with their Ipad, and the extra capabilities. Drawing users love Undo/Redo and drawing percision. 
4. How does your Pen help you use Color (Re-word)
    Note users enjoy just needing one pen that has digital color. Drawing users say their drawing software which is enabled with a pen is great.
6. Have you had form factor problems with a smart Pen (shape / hand fatiuge / etc)
    Users have no issue at all with their hands.
7. What apps do you use with your Smart Pen
    Note users primarly ony use one app. Drawing users may use a couple different apps. 
8. If you could add one feature to pen
    Note users would like a back that can erase. (Highly saught after). Drawing users want more percision and better compatability

## Generated Requirements
These needs are not ordered. They come from the numbered question answers.

1. On-Pen controls, Pressure function, solid weight
2. programmable controls, magnetic
3. Accurating drawing feature lines
4. Pen can quickly change colors, works with different software.
5. works as smart pen
6. Has standard form factor / weight distribution
7. Notes and Drawing users can enjoy the pen.
8. Eraser / gesture functionality on back.
